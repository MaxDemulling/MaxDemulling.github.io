Hoofbeat just around the corner in Cadott Wisconsin

What is Hoofbeat?

Hoofbeat is a country music and camping festival located in Cadott, Wisconsin, and is one of the largest in the U.S., lasting over three days. The grounds contain over 7,500 campsites and multiple stages. A fan stated,this is the best music festival that I have evr been to and will definitely be back.

How did it begin?

Hoofbeat, also known as Country Fest, began in 1987 as a three-day country music and camping event in Cadott, Wisconsin. The grounds were founded by four people, Duane Crank, Mike Asher, Lyle Goettl, and Ronnie Hager.

How is it doing today?

In the present day Hoofbeat has had over 90,000 to 100,000 people visit to either watch or camp. 2026 will be the 39th Hoofbeat/Country Fest with many more to come. Hoofbeat is a very well-known festival is the U.S. and will probably expand in the future!
